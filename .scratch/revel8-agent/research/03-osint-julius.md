He speaks at it-sa, runs founder-led content, and just moved to Munich amid an active startup scene. An
"invitation to keynote / join a panel / record a podcast on AI social engineering" (especially tied to a

Munich move. Strong because it also provides a natural reason to send a "briefing doc," calendar link, or

revel8 is hiring aggressively in Munich (many Founders Associate, Head of Marketing, Enterprise AE, interns).
A pretext as a high-caliber candidate, a recruiter with a "senior GTM leader," or a referral touches a real

**Top pick:** **H1 + H2** — a warm, credible **enterprise/CISO lead or a Munich/deepfake-themed speaking-press
